## Cross-model comparison

| model | phase 1 wall p50 | VRAM | VRAM/4GB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| smolvlm short | 181 ms | 998 MiB | 24% |
| smolvlm medium | 3798 ms | 1001 MiB | 24% |
| smolvlm long | 3918 ms | 1003 MiB | 24% |
| smolvla | 1501 ms | 2021 MiB | 49% |
| sd-turbo | 535 ms | 3098 MiB | 76% |

- **smolvla is the most VRAM-heavy** (2021 MiB vs smolvlm's 1003 MiB) — the extra ~1 GB is the action expert weights (SigLIP + SmolVLM2 + flow-matching denoiser). The 4 GB ceiling is the binding constraint: larger batch or more flow steps would OOM.
- **smolvla wall (1501 ms) < smolvlm medium/long (3798-3918 ms)** despite being a 2-stage pipeline. The reason: smolvla runs 10 flow-matching steps (small per-step cost), while smolvlm runs 64 AR token decodes (larger per-step cost for the same LM). The VLM prefix in smolvla is ~80 tokens (one-shot, not AR).
- **sd-turbo (535 ms) is cheapest** but uses most VRAM (UNet + VAE decoders).

## Initial observation

- **smolvla VRAM is the bottleneck** at 49%. Two flow-matching stages with 10 steps each, plus the full policy (SigLIP + SmolVLM2 + action expert). Fits in 4 GB only at batch=1, 10 steps.
- **1501 ms is acceptable for a robot action chunk** (the next action is computed in 1.5s, which is within typical robot control loop budgets of 2-10 Hz).
- **Synthetic input caveat**: real LIBERO state + image would produce different wall times (different image encoding cost). This profile is a proxy for the pipeline overhead, not a real-world benchmark.

## Bottleneck hypothesis

- **VRAM-constrained** (49% usage → room for 2x batch). Not compute-bound: the per-flow-step cost is small (1500 / 10 = ~150 ms per step).
- **VLM prefix forward** (SigLIP + SmolVLM2 embed_prefix + vlm_with_expert.forward) likely dominates the first ~30% of the 1501 ms. NVTX per-stage breakdown is needed to confirm.
