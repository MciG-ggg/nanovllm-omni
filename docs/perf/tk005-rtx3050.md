## Bench re-measurement (RTX 3050)

### Full three-stage E2E (production path, `--pipeline full`)

`bench time --pipeline full` routes one prompt end-to-end through
`Omni.generate` (thinker → talker → MTP → Mimi → WAV) on real weights.
All six prompts × 20 runs, torch 2.14.0+cu130, `8f6d522` + bench full path:

| prompt_id | total_median (ms) | total_p95 (ms) | total_min (ms) | frames | vram_peak (MiB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| medium_01 | 747 | 844 | 691 | 15 | 1882 |
| medium_02 | 767 | 850 | 699 | 15 | 1883 |
| short_01 | 645 | 709 | 603 | 10 | 1881 |
| short_02 | 628 | 661 | 592 | 9 | 1881 |
| short_03 | 634 | 669 | 604 | 10 | 1881 |
| system_01 | 1038 | 1109 | 818 | 15 | 1881 |

**628–1038 ms total median across prompts; p95 ≤ 1110 ms; VRAM peak
~1881 MiB.** Raw per-run data: `docs/perf/full-e2e-rtx3050.csv`.

### Thinker-decode primitive (regression only, NOT E2E)

The default `bench time` measures the thinker decode stage alone
(`run_generate`), which the README previously quoted as end-to-end:

| Path | runs | total median range (ms) | frames | VRAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--use-thinker-cuda-graph` | 20/prompt | 173–182 | 16 | ~1180 MiB |
| eager (default) | 10/prompt | 457–553 | 8 | ~615 MiB |

This is ~4–6× faster than full E2E because it excludes the talker/MTP and
Mimi/encode stages. It stays in the bench as a stage-regression primitive.

The prior README figures (~320 ms / ~715 ms) were measured on torch
2.13.0+cu130; both the thinker primitive and full E2E were re-measured on
torch 2.14.
