## Initial observation

- **No single hot kernel.** Workload is balanced across cudnn format converters (NCHW<->NHWC, ~1240 events), cutlass GEMM (UNet conv, 465), and at:: layer_norm / elementwise / RowwiseMomentsCUDAKernel (~1800 events combined).
- **VRAM at 76% of 4 GB ceiling** -- memory pressure is real. Larger resolution or longer runs would OOM.
- **Per-run variance is small** (p99-p50 = 4.6 ms / 535 ms ~= 0.9%). Workload is steady-state after warmup.

## Bottleneck hypothesis (preliminary)

Based on kernel distribution + memory utilization, sd-turbo on RTX 3050 is most likely **compute-bound** (UNet conv dominates event time) with **memory pressure as a secondary constraint** (76% VRAM). CUDA Graph capture could reduce per-launch overhead, but 86k events / 5 runs = ~17k events/run suggests launch overhead is significant too -- need per-stage breakdown to confirm.

## Phase 2 plan

1. **Add NVTX per-stage markers** to `_infer_one` in `bench_sd_turbo.py` (`:tokenize`, `:unet`, `:vae-decode`). Re-profile to split the 534.7 ms into the 3 stages.
2. **Colab T4 ncu SOL** for the top-3 kernels (cudnn NCHW<->NHWC, cutlass GEMM, at:: layer_norm). WSL 3050 can't collect counters.
3. **Cell matrix design** once per-stage wall + ncu SOL are available. Initial guess: `(CG on/off) x (precision fp16/fp32)` -- 4 cells.
